01 · THE BRAND
RYZN BRAND GUIDELINES

What Ryzn is

Ryzn is an invitation-only mentorship network where access is earned, not bought. Mentees prove commitment before they can reach a mentor. Mentors keep small, deliberate circles. The product exists in two forms: a consumer network, and Ryzn for Teams, the same engine deployed inside a company.

Positioning

Premium, disciplined, and warm. Closer to a members club than a marketplace. We never describe mentorship as a transaction, a booking, or a subscription to someone's time.

Tagline

Rise now.

Always two words, sentence case, full stop. Set in Space Mono uppercase when used as a label inside product surfaces: RISE NOW.

The three promises

Earned. Stage 1 comes before contact. The gate is the product.

Small. Mentors cap their circles. Scarcity is a feature, never an apology.

Proven. Progress is recorded, verifiable, and portable. Badges mean something because they cannot be bought.

Naming

Ryzn in running text. RYZN in the wordmark and mono labels. Never Rise'n, RYZEN, or Ryzn.io. The mentor circle is an Orbit. The community is The Roster.

02 · THE MARK
RYZN BRAND GUIDELINES

Ascent

Three diamonds, growing as they climb. The diamond is the atom of the product: it marks tiers, bullets, and badges throughout the interface. Repeated three times at increasing scale it becomes the story of the brand, which is a person getting measurably bigger over time. The mark is always drawn from these coordinates. Do not redraw it by eye.

Construction

Each diamond is a square rotated 45 degrees, defined by a centre and a radius. Radii step at a constant ratio of roughly 1.36, so the three shapes read as one sequence rather than three sizes.

D1  c(11, 70)  r 11
D2  c(40, 48)  r 15
D3  c(69, 20)  r 20
ART BOX  89 × 81

The atom

At sizes below 32 px the three diamonds blur together. Use the single diamond instead. It is the same shape at the same angle, so the brand still reads.

04 · CLEAR SPACE AND SIZE
RYZN BRAND GUIDELINES

Give it room

Clear space on all four sides equals X, the height of the smallest diamond in the mark. Nothing enters that zone: no type, no rules, no photography, no page edge.

Minimum sizes

HORIZONTAL  120 PX / 32 MM
STACKED  80 PX / 22 MM
MARK  24 PX / 7 MM
ATOM  16 PX

Backgrounds

Purple or ink on white and surface. White on ink, purple, deep, and photography that has been darkened to at least 55 percent.

Duotone rule

Duotone is for light backgrounds only. Lilac fails contrast on white for text but is acceptable as the smallest shape in the mark, where it is decorative.

05 · MISUSE
RYZN BRAND GUIDELINES

Ten things that break the logo

Do not recolour

No gradients, no brand-adjacent purples, no division colours applied to the mark.

Do not rotate or shear

The diamonds are already at 45 degrees. Rotating the lockup destroys the ascent.

Do not restack

Never rearrange the three diamonds, change their spacing, or add a fourth.

Do not outline

The mark is solid. No strokes, no hollow shapes, no drop shadows.

Do not stretch

Scale proportionally. Always.

Do not retype the wordmark

Use the supplied outlines. Space Grotesk set live will not match the tracking.

Do not add effects

No bevels, glows, or animation that deforms the shapes. Fade and scale only.

Do not crowd

Respect X on all sides, including against the edge of a slide or screen.

Do not place on busy photography

Darken the image or use a solid block behind the logo.

Do not use the mark as a bullet at text size

Use the single diamond for that. It is the same motif, built for the job.

Rules that follow from the table

Coral and amber never carry body copy on white. Use them for chips, bars, icons, and large numerals, or darken them for text.

Lilac and mute are never text on white. Lilac is for marks on dark backgrounds and decorative shapes. Mute is for placeholder and metadata on surface only at large sizes.

Safe defaults

Ink on white for body. Gray on white for secondary. White on purple for primary buttons. Teal on teal tint for success states. Purple on purple tint for informational chips.

Every one of those pairs clears 4.5 to 1.

08 · TYPOGRAPHY
RYZN BRAND GUIDELINES

Two faces, strict roles

Aa

Space Grotesk

Everything a human reads as language: headlines, body, buttons, names. Weights 300 to 700. Headlines are 700 with tracking at minus 0.02em. Body is 400 or 500 at 1.6 line height.

OFL 1.1 · BUNDLED IN /FONTS

Aa

Space Mono

Everything the system says about itself: labels, metadata, codes, stats, timestamps, section kickers. Always uppercase, always tracked at 0.12em or wider, never below 6.5 pt equivalent and never for sentences.

OFL 1.1 · BUNDLED IN /FONTS

Scale

Display 34 / 1.02
Heading 20 / 1.15
Subhead 13 / 1.3
Body 9.5 to 11 at 1.6 line height, gray or ink
LABEL 8 UPPERCASE TRACKED 0.14EM

Do

Set numbers and codes in mono. Let headlines run tight and short. Keep paragraph measure under 75 characters.

Do not

Set body copy in mono. Mix a third typeface. Use Century Gothic or Consolas except as the declared fallbacks in decks and email.

11 · VOICE
RYZN BRAND GUIDELINES

How Ryzn sounds

Direct, warm, and slightly understated. We are talking to ambitious people who are tired of being sold to. Short sentences. Concrete nouns. No hype.

We say

"Earn your way in."   "Three questions before you apply."   "Your mentor sees this."   "Week 6 of 12."   "Rise now."

Second person. Present tense. Numbers where they are real.

We never say

"Unlock your potential."   "Book a session."   "Hire a mentor."   "Supercharge your career."   "Join thousands of users."

No growth-hack vocabulary, no transaction language, no vanity scale.

House style

  • No em dashes or en dashes anywhere. Use a full stop, a comma, or a colon. This is absolute.
  • Sentence case for headlines and buttons. Uppercase only for mono labels.
  • Canadian English. Organisation and colour in brand copy, but keep product code in US spelling.
  • Numerals for everything countable. Week 6, 3 questions, 12 weeks.
  • Never call a person a user in anything a person will read. They are mentees, mentors, members.
  • The full stop in "Rise now." is part of the tagline. Keep it.
